Executive Summary & Vision

Sahayak (सहायक) is an institutional-grade, AI-driven, hyper-local business advisory and credit structuring platform architected specifically for India's 63+ million rural micro-entrepreneurs (small shopkeepers, artisans, weavers, dairy farmers, food processors, and agrarian businesses). It pays special affirmative focus to underprivileged beneficiaries under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE)—including Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Other Backward Classes (OBC), and rural women entrepreneurs.

Operating as a voice-first, dialect-aware Progressive Web App (PWA) supporting 10 regional Indian dialects, Sahayak bridges the gap between unbanked micro-enterprises and formal credit institutions, completely eradicating predatory informal moneylenders charging 36% to 120% APR.

02

Ground Realities & Problem Definition (PS ID: SIH26091)

📉

₹14 Lakh Crore Credit Deficit

As cited by the RBI UK Sinha Committee, rural micro-enterprises operate in the cash economy without audited financials or collateral, leading to over 78% bank rejection.

⛓️

Predatory Debt Traps (36%–120% APR)

Denied formal credit, rural entrepreneurs turn to local loan sharks charging extortionate daily/weekly compounding interest rates, trapping whole families in cyclical debt.

📑

Scheme Complexity & Intermediaries

Massive capital subsidies (PMEGP, NSFDC, MUDRA) go unclaimed due to digital illiteracy, English text-heavy forms, and predatory tout commissions.

🌾

Rigid EMIs vs. Seasonal Harvests

Commercial banks mandate rigid monthly payments. Rural producers face dry cashflows during sowing months and windfall income post-harvest, causing unnecessary technical default.

04

4-Module Core Product Architecture

📍

Module 1: Hyper-Local Catchment & Mandi Price Intelligence

• Real-time HTML5 GPS geocoding against nearest DIC and APMC mandis.
• Mandi Wholesale Price Index tracking wholesale commodity prices.
• Algorithmic competition density and local viability scoring.

🎙️

Module 2: Dialect-First Conversational AI Advisory

• Hands-free bi-directional voice in 10 regional Indian dialects.
• 8 specialized rural trade archetypes (Dairy, Handloom, Kirana, Agri).
• Google Gemini 1.5 Flash rural-trade fine-tuning + static offline fallback.

⚖️

Module 3: Deterministic Scheme Structuring Engine

• Zero financial hallucinations: Decoupled mathematical rules engine.
• PMEGP capital subsidy computation (15%, 25%, and 35% Special category).
• MoSJE NSFDC 6% apex loan vs. 8.5% bank channel comparison.

📊

Module 4: Smart Cashflow Ledger & Bank-Ready DPR

• Offline-first persistent voice ledger (Cash In, Expense, Customer Udhar).
• 1-Click WhatsApp payment reminders with UPI deep-links.
• Harvest/Yield-Aware Seasonal Repayment Equalizer + Bank DPR PDF export.
