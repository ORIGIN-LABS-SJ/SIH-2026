  • • Voice-guided UI eliminates digital literacy barriers

⚠️ Risk 1: Financial Hallucination HIGH SEVERITY
Mitigation: Decoupled architecture. LLM handles vernacular explanation; strict deterministic mathematical engine calculates EMI and interest rates.
✓ Outcome: Zero financial calculation errors (100% deterministic accuracy)
📶 Risk 2: Rural Internet Fluctuations ENVIRONMENT
Mitigation: Progressive Web App (PWA) with client-side caching. Low-bandwidth JSON payload (<50 KB) with offline queue sync.
✓ Outcome: Works seamlessly offline and on 2G / fluctuating networks
🤝 Risk 3: Adoption & Cultural Distrust BEHAVIORAL
Mitigation: Hyper-local dialect support (Marwari, Marathi, Gujarati) + Common Service Centres (CSCs) and SHG facilitators.
✓ Outcome: Community-verified trust and rapid grassroots adoption
